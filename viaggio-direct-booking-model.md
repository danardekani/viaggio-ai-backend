## What Direct Booking Actually Means

### You Become an Online Travel Agency (OTA)

```
AFFILIATE MODEL (Simple)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

User → Viaggio → "Book on Booking.com" → Booking.com handles everything
                        │
                        └── You get 5% commission, done.


DIRECT BOOKING MODEL (Complex)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

User → Viaggio → Checkout on Viaggio → You handle:
                        │
                        ├── Payment processing (Stripe)
                        ├── Booking confirmation
                        ├── Supplier payment (hotel/airline)
                        ├── E-tickets / vouchers
                        ├── Customer support
                        ├── Refunds & cancellations
                        ├── Fraud prevention
                        ├── Legal compliance
                        └── 24/7 operations
```

## Decision Framework

### Stay Affiliate If:
- Solo founder or tiny team
- Bootstrap budget (<$10K)
- Goal is side income ($50-200K/year)
- Risk-averse
- No travel industry experience

### Go Direct Booking If:
- Have raised capital ($500K+)
- Team of 5+ people
- Goal is venture-scale ($10M+)
- Willing to handle operations complexity
- Want to own customer relationship

### Hybrid Approach If:
- Small team (2-4 people)
- Moderate budget ($50-200K)
- Goal is sustainable business ($1-5M)
- Want to test before committing
- Building toward eventual direct model
